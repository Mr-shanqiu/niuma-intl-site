Frequently asked

What can I buy?

Three one-time options: client 9.6 for US$0.80; appearances for US$0.20 each, bought with the client or from 4 at a time (4 = US$0.80); or the Complete pack for US$5.88 with the client, all current appearances, one VIP appearance and all future appearances.

Can I buy a single appearance on its own?

No. Appearances are bought together with the client, or from 4 at a time (4 = US$0.80). A single appearance is not sold alone.

How do I receive the app after buying?

Right after payment we email you the download link for Windows or macOS together with your appearance files. Keep that email so you can re-download.

Does it read what I type?

No. It only counts that a key press or click happened. Content, cursor position, window titles and clipboard are never read or stored.

Does it need an account or internet?

No. It runs fully offline, with no login, activation or telemetry. All counts and settings stay on your own computer.

Can I get a refund?

This is digital content delivered instantly, so we do not refund a change of mind. If a download or import fails, or you were charged twice, contact us within 30 days and we will fix it or refund you.

Which systems are supported?

Windows 10/11 and macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel).
