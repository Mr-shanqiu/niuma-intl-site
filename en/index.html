Frequently asked

Does it read what I type?

No. It only counts that a key press or click happened. Content, cursor position, window titles and clipboard are never read or stored.

Does it need an account or internet?

No. The client runs fully offline. There is no login, activation or telemetry.

Where is my data stored?

All counts, settings and curves stay on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded.
